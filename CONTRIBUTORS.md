## 🤝 Community Contributions
This project is **100% Free & Open Source (FOSS)** under the MIT License.  
We warmly welcome contributions from the Linux community worldwide!

### How to Contribute:
1. **Fork** the repository: `https://github.com/TheJPlay2006/waybar-sekiro`
2. **Create a branch** for your feature or distro optimization: `git checkout -b feature/my-feature`
3. **Commit** your enhancements: `git commit -m 'feat: improve multi-distro support'`
4. **Push** to GitHub and open a **Pull Request**!
